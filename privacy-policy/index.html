Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 14 October 2026

At Heart Symbols, we believe web tools should respect your digital privacy completely. This policy outlines our straightforward, transparent data practices in plain English.

1. No User Accounts

You can use every feature of this website immediately without signing up, logging in, or providing any personal details. We do not maintain user databases or password records.

2. Zero Cookies and Storage Tracking

We do not set cookies on your web browser. We do not use storage to build user profiles, track browsing behaviors, or identify returning visitors. When you build a string in the "Your mix" tool, that text is held temporarily in volatile memory within your active browser tab and is discarded when the tab closes.

3. No Analytics or Third-Party Tracking

At launch, Heart Symbols operates with zero third-party scripts, zero analytics suites, zero advertising networks, and zero tracking pixels. No data is transmitted to external tracking companies or marketing platforms.

4. Local Clipboard Actions

When you click or tap a heart symbol button or the "Copy all" button, your browser writes that text to your system clipboard using standard browser APIs (navigator.clipboard or standard selection fallback). The site never reads from your clipboard, never accesses previous clipboard contents, and never transmits your copied text across the network.

5. Policy Updates

If our operational practices or technical features ever change in the future, this page will be updated immediately with a revised effective date.

6. Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy statement, you can reach us via our contact page.
