What are heart symbols?

Heart symbols are characters you can type, copy, and paste like any other text. The most common are ♥ (black heart suit), ♡ (white heart suit), ❤ (heavy black heart), and ❣ (heavy heart exclamation mark ornament). Each one is a single Unicode character, so it works in usernames, bios, captions, messages, and documents without an image.

Text hearts take on the color of the text around them: dark text gives a dark heart, and colored text gives a colored heart. Heart emoji such as ❤️ 💛 💜 are different. They are small colored pictures drawn by your device, so they keep their color but look a little different on Apple, Google, Microsoft, and Samsung devices.

Heart symbols vs heart emoji: what is the difference?

While both represent hearts, text symbols and emoji differ in color behavior, platform rendering, and system compatibility.

Comparison between text heart symbols and heart emoji
Feature Text heart symbols (♥ ♡ ❤) Heart emoji (❤️ 💜 🩷)

Some sites restrict special characters of both kinds, so check the platform's rules before you save a username.

How to type a heart symbol

Windows

Turn on Num Lock, hold Alt, and type 3 on the numeric keypad to enter ♥. For emoji, press the Windows key and the period key (Win + .) to open the emoji panel and search for 'heart'.

Mac

There is no default single-key shortcut for ♥. Press Control + Command + Space to open Emoji & Symbols, then search for 'heart'.

iPhone and iPad

Switch to the emoji keyboard with the globe or smiley key and pick a heart. For text hearts such as ♡ or ♥, copy them from this page.

Android

Open the emoji key on Gboard or your keyboard and search for 'heart'. For text hearts, copy them from this page.

Web developers

Use the HTML entities, CSS escapes, or JavaScript escapes from the table above. Add the invisible character U+FE0E (&#xFE0E;) after ❤ to ask for the plain text style, or U+FE0F (&#xFE0F;) to ask for the emoji style. Support depends on the font and the platform.

Why does my heart symbol look different or show as a box?

Each device picks a font to draw a symbol. If that font does not include the character, you may see an empty box or a different-looking heart.

  • Use the most widely supported hearts first: ♥ ♡ ❤.
  • If ❤ appears as a red emoji and you want a plain one, paste ❤︎ (❤ followed by U+FE0E) from the text hearts group.
  • Newer emoji such as 🩷 🩵 🩶 appear as boxes on older systems. Updating the operating system usually fixes it.
  • Decorative symbols are drawn by different fonts, so the same heart can look thicker, thinner, or tilted from app to app.

Where you can use heart symbols

Heart symbols add personality across personal profiles, social media bios, nicknames, captions, comments, and direct messages. In documents, simple text hearts like ❥ and ❧ serve as elegant decorative bullet points or section dividers. Rules differ by platform, and some username fields block special characters.

Frequently asked questions

What are heart symbols?

Heart symbols are Unicode characters shaped like hearts, such as ♥, ♡, ❤, and ❣. You can copy and paste them like normal text.

How do I copy and paste a heart symbol?

Tap or click a heart on this page and it is copied to your clipboard. Then paste it where you want it with Ctrl + V, Command + V, or by long-pressing on a phone.

How do I type a heart symbol on my keyboard?

On Windows, hold Alt and press 3 on the numeric keypad for ♥. On Mac and phones there is no direct shortcut for text hearts, so use the Emoji & Symbols viewer or copy the heart from this page.

What is the difference between ♥ and ♡?

♥ is the black (solid) heart suit and ♡ is the white (outline) heart suit. Both are text symbols that take your text color.

What is the difference between ❤ and ❤️?

❤ (U+2764) is the heavy black heart character, which many systems draw as a plain text symbol. ❤️ is the same character followed by U+FE0F, which asks the device to show the red emoji version.

Why does a heart symbol show as a box?

The font in use does not contain that character. Try a more common heart such as ♥ or ♡, or update your device.

Can I use heart symbols in usernames and bios?

Often yes, but every platform sets its own rules about special characters. If a symbol is rejected, try another heart or an emoji.

What is the Unicode and HTML code for the heart symbol?

The black heart suit ♥ is U+2665. In HTML you can write &hearts; or &#9829;. The white heart suit ♡ is U+2661, written &#9825;.

Are these heart symbols free to use?

Yes. The characters are part of the Unicode standard and are free to use in personal and commercial text.
